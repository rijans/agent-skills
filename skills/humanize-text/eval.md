# Humanize Text Evaluation

Before returning substantial prose:
- Natural and specific rather than generic or formulaic.
- Meaning, facts, uncertainty, intent, and voice preserved.
- Nothing invented.
- Generic AI and corporate filler removed.
- Repetition removed.
- Sentence and paragraph structures are not mechanically repetitive.
- Headings, lists, introductions, and conclusions are used only when useful.
- Language is no more complicated than necessary.
- Tone fits the author, audience, and format.
- No fake anecdotes, experiences, opinions, emotions, or imperfections.

## Edits
- Minimum effective changes.
- Preserve strong original sentences.
- Keep the writer's recognizable voice.

## Drafts
- Follow the brief and audience.
- Do not invent personal experience or unsupported claims.
- Let structure serve the content.

## Detection
- Identify observable patterns only.
- Quote relevant text.
- Never claim to know whether AI wrote the text.
- Never assign an AI score.

## Final question
Would this read naturally if a knowledgeable person had written it directly?
If not, rewrite the problematic sentence or paragraph.
